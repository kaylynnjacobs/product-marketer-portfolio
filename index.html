GoTyme x SAB - Product GTM

From launch data to a redesigned funding journey

Partner Marketing Specialist · GTM lead
+

After launching a new white-label funding product for SAB's tavern-owner merchant base, leadership needed a clear read on what was and wasn't working across the funnel — and a recommendation for what to change next.

What I built
  • A multi-channel launch comms plan (push notifications, SMS, in-app banners, and warm-up calls), each mapped to a specific funnel stage and objective
  • A full-funnel post-campaign analysis tracking the journey from first comms through session, digital case, application, and disbursement
  • A set of root-cause insights showing that data quality, application friction, and comms frequency/visibility — not merchant demand — were the main constraints on performance
What I did
  • Owned the GTM for the launch and the post-campaign analysis that followed it
  • Translated those findings into a forward-looking recommendation: moving from a high-touch application journey to a low-touch, API-integrated journey embedded directly into the partner's own app
Result: turned a new product launch's performance data into a concrete, lower-friction journey redesign recommendation for the next phase of the partnership, not just a performance readout.

GoTyme x Partners - Analysis and Planning

Scaling one lifecycle framework across a multi-partner portfolio

Partner Marketing Specialist · Mr D Business Funding & Netcash Instant Funding
+

Each partner portfolio had a year of campaign history but no shared way to compare performance, understand why segments behaved differently, or decide what to do next — every partner review was starting from scratch.

What I built
  • One consistent lifecycle framework applied across multiple portfolios: segmenting merchants into New, Readvance/Top-up, and Dormant (plus Industry for one portfolio), each measured against the same three metrics — open rate (awareness), click rate (interest), and click-to-open rate (consideration)
  • A campaign audit identifying which subject lines, messaging angles, and CTAs performed best in each segment, and why: New merchants need trust and simplicity, Readvance/Top-up merchants respond to momentum and reward, Dormant merchants need urgency and reassurance the offer still stands
  • A shared 2026 roadmap across both partners: merchant personas and surveys, a standardised quarterly campaign cadence, a social-proof content bank, and WhatsApp as a new channel
What I did
  • Owned the 2025 performance review and 2026 strategy for each portfolio, adapting the same core framework to each partner's merchant base and data
  • Presented findings and the forward roadmap per portfolio to leadership
Result: replaced separate, ad hoc portfolio reviews with one repeatable strategic model — making each new partner portfolio faster to analyse and easier to benchmark against the others.
GoTyme x Takealot - Product UX and Messaging

Rebuilding a funding offer screen around what sellers actually said

Partner Marketing Specialist · Seller Capital
+

Sellers were dropping off between the first and second screen of the Seller Capital funding offer, and the business needed to know exactly why before redesigning anything.

What I built
  • Analysis of a 424-respondent seller survey that identified the two biggest clarity gaps: repayment mechanics (the single largest gap) and total cost of credit
  • A redesigned offer screen showing total cost of credit up front, a plain-language "holdback rate" label with tooltip in place of confusing terminology, a dedicated "how does repayment actually work?" explainer, an extended repayment-term range, and a 60-second explainer video
  • A post-application expectation ("Seller Portal + email, ~2 days") set directly from the survey's confirmed channel preference, instead of the calls sellers said they didn't want
What I did
  • Mapped every survey finding directly to a specific screen element and wrote the plain-language copy and tooltips
  • Led a follow-up working session reordering the screen and catching a placeholder disbursement figure before it shipped, replacing it with GoTyme Bank's actual published numbers
Result: replaced jargon and guesswork with a redesign grounded in direct feedback from 424 sellers — and caught a data-accuracy issue before it reached production.
